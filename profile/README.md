# Astrophysics with Large Astronomical Surveys (ALAS)

ALAS is a research group based at the Instituto de Astrofísica de La Plata (IALP, CONICET–UNLP) and the Facultad de Ciencias Astronómicas y Geofísicas (FCAG–UNLP), Argentina.

We study galactic and extragalactic astrophysics using multiband photometric surveys, including S-PLUS, J-PLUS, J-PAS, the DESI Legacy Imaging Surveys and SDSS, together with cosmological simulations such as Illustris-TNG and EAGLE. Our methods combine big-data analysis, machine learning and automated object classification.

ALAS is part of the S-PLUS (Southern Photometric Local Universe Survey) collaboration.
